## Monitoring Framework

### Amplitude Scale (0-100)
- **0-10**: Mechanism present but not escalating (baseline)
- **10-30**: Slow escalation; system adaptive capacity adequate
- **30-50**: Rapid escalation; system at inflection point
- **50+**: Runaway escalation; system in crisis-response mode only

### Confidence Levels
- **HIGH**: Multiple independent data sources, peer-reviewed documentation, institutional verification
- **MODERATE**: Documented evidence but limited geographic/sectoral coverage or time series
- **SPECULATIVE**: Plausible but limited evidence; under observation

### Measurement Stability Status
- **STABLE**: Measurement systems functioning; long time series available; no disruption
- **DEGRADING**: Measurement system under stress; gaps emerging; some regions losing monitoring
- **FAILED**: Measurement systems down; data gaps; blind spots expanding
